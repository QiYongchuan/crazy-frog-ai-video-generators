## Questions to ask before choosing a generator

### Is a Crazy Frog AI video generator free?

The Crazy Frog AI listed first here charges for generation; its first video is $7.99. Do not assume that a tool is free because it has a free-to-open upload page. Read the actual checkout terms for any service.

### Does the downloaded video include Axel F?

The Crazy Frog AI download includes engine/chase effects, without the original song. Sound policies differ across the tools above, so check the chosen service and the actual download rather than assuming its website examples represent the paid file's audio.

### Do I need to write a prompt or upload the original video?

For The Crazy Frog AI, neither is required: upload one rider photo; the scene and generation setup are built in. General-purpose video models may require reference assets and their own prompting workflow.

### What kind of photo should I use?

Use a sharp, well-lit photo showing one face clearly. Avoid tiny faces, heavy blur and face coverings. A clearer input can help, but AI-generated faces and movements can still vary. Follow each service's photo and consent rules.

### Which tool is best?

Choose according to the controls and output you need. The Crazy Frog AI offers a fixed-scene workflow; other tools in this list advertise additional controls. This list does not establish a universal winner or guarantee a particular result.
